# CSAT Measurement Policy

## How it is collected

A one-question survey is sent on ticket closure, scored 1 to 5. Only closed
tickets are surveyed, so open tickets have no CSAT value and must be excluded
from any average — including them as zeros is the most common reporting error.

## Targets

The company target is a mean CSAT of 4.0 or above. Below 3.5 for two consecutive
months on any single plan tier opens a committed remediation item.

## What counts as a bad score

A 1 or 2 is a detractor and triggers a follow-up within one business day. A 3 is
neutral and is not followed up, which is deliberate: chasing neutrals produced no
measurable score movement in the 2025 review and consumed about 6% of agent time.

## Known bias

Self-serve customers respond at roughly a third of the Enterprise rate, so their
mean is noisier and should not be compared directly against Enterprise without
saying so. Response-rate weighting was tried and rejected as harder to explain
than the raw number.
