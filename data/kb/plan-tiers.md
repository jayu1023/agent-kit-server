# Plan Tiers

## Enterprise

Annual contract, named account team, 24/7 support, customer-managed encryption
keys, and a separate cluster available for regulatory isolation. Two tenants
currently use the isolated cluster.

## Business

Monthly or annual, business-hours support, standard retention, no
customer-managed keys. This is the volume tier by ticket count.

## Self-serve

Credit card, no contract, business-hours support with a one-business-day response
target. Self-serve tickets are predominantly how-to questions rather than
defects, which is why their first-response times are the longest and their
escalation rate is the lowest.

## Tier changes

An upgrade takes effect immediately; a downgrade takes effect at the end of the
billing period. A downgrade from Enterprise releases the isolated cluster only
after a 30-day retention window.
