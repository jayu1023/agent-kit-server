# Internal Reporting Standards

## Rounding and units

Minutes for first response, hours for resolution. Report one decimal place, never
more — a first-response average quoted to three decimals implies a precision the
sample size does not support.

## Excluding open tickets

Any metric involving resolution time or CSAT must exclude open tickets. Any metric
about volume or first response must include them. Mixing the two populations in
one table without a note is the most common review finding.

## Segmenting

Always segment by plan before drawing a conclusion. Aggregate support numbers are
dominated by the Business tier because it has the most tickets, so the aggregate
mean usually describes Business and nothing else.

## Comparisons

Month-over-month comparisons need the same number of business days or they are not
comparable. Quote the n alongside any mean; a mean over fewer than 10 tickets is
reported as a range, not a point.
